# Arsenal FC

## cannon.png provenance

`assets/cannon.png` is derived from **Arsenal F.C.'s own 1921–22 club crest**
— "the crest is a westward pointing single cannon," adopted for one season
and then replaced. It is in the public domain (published before 1931; see
the file's own licensing block) and is documented as uncopyrighted by
Arsenal's own crest history:

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arsenal_F.C._Crest_1921%E2%80%931922.png
- Citing: https://www.arsenal.com/news/news-archive/the-arsenal-crest

The original is a detailed line illustration; at this panel's pixel budget
it was thresholded to a single color and reduced to 48x22 px
(majority-vote per cell, no anti-aliasing, no interpolation) so it draws as
clean pixel art rather than a blurred photo — 48px wide is roughly the
minimum where the wheel spokes and barrel stay legible. The lit pixels are
Arsenal red (#EF0107) on a transparent ground, so the mark stands directly
on the app's black panel. The modern shield
crest (with the "Arsenal" wordmark, gold trim, and three colored panels)
was tried first and does not survive reduction to panel resolution — it
becomes an unreadable smear even at 24x24px — which is why this simpler,
single-element historical crest was used instead. The 64px classic build
(`apps/arsenal-fc`) only has room for a much smaller ~20px rendition of the
same source, which reads as a rougher silhouette than this one.

Wikimedia's file page notes that although the image itself is out of
copyright, "this image shows ... an official insignia" and related
trademark rights are independent of copyright status and vary by
jurisdiction.
